What We Do

SecurityRegistry.us is a posting and lifecycle registry platform designed for broker-dealers and issuers to manage exempt securities offerings under Florida Invest Local and federal exemptions.

  • Issuer Transparency: Offerings are posted with full disclosure and jurisdictional tagging.
  • Broker-Dealer Governance: Only registered broker-dealers manage RFQs and investor onboarding.
  • Immutable Lifecycle Logging: All offering events are recorded for audit-traceable transparency.
  • No Matching or Execution: SecurityRegistry.us does not facilitate trade execution or custody.

Regulatory Clarity

SecurityRegistry.us is not an ECN, ATS, or securities exchange. It does not match orders, execute trades, or hold customer assets. All transactions are conducted off-platform by registered broker-dealers or through National Market System (NMS) exchanges, in full accordance with applicable state and federal regulations. The platform serves solely as a lifecycle registry for offering events, RFQ postings, and immutable transaction records.

Future Integration

The ELMX Exchange will utilize SecurityRegistry.us as its immutable transaction ledger, anchoring both exempt and public offerings with full lifecycle traceability, smart contract governance, and CAT/FIX reporting—without altering the registry’s core role as a broker-dealer–driven recordkeeping platform.
